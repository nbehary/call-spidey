# Call Spidey

A private, on-device app that lets a child place a pretend phone call to
Spidey. Vocabulary: `CONTEXT.md`. Decisions: `docs/adr/`.

Two platforms share this repo:

- **Android** (the repo root: `app/`, Gradle files, `tools/`). Finished and
  working. State: `docs/STATE.md`. Snapshot: `docs/HANDOFF.md`.
- **iOS** (`ios/`). A port in progress, done as a learning exercise.
  **Before doing anything for iOS, read `ios/CLAUDE.md`** — the owner writes
  the Swift and you tutor. Then `docs/ios/PROGRESS.md` for where things
  stand, and `docs/ios/PLAN.md` for the plan.
